Delete Your Account

You can delete your The Long Game account and associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: In the app

  • Open The Long Game and sign in
  • Go to Profile → tap the gear icon → Settings
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm

This immediately deletes your login credentials and push notification data. Your past picks stay on file in anonymized form so that leaderboards and head-to-head records for other users remain accurate — no personal information is retained.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have access to the app, email us and we'll delete your account for you.

nickcorum@gmail.com

Include the email address associated with your account. Requests are processed within a few days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login credentials (email/password, or Google/Apple sign-in link)
  • Your push notification tokens
  • Your name and profile information (replaced with a generic placeholder)

See our Privacy Policy for more on how your data is handled.
