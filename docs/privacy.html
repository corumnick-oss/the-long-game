Privacy Policy

The Long Game — Last updated August 16, 2026

The Long Game ("the app," "we," "us") is an NFL picks and leaderboard app open to anyone who wants to join. "The Gridirons" is a private friend group within the app with its own leaderboard view, but the app itself and its global leaderboard are open to any user. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. The app is for entertainment purposes only — it does not involve real-money wagering, gambling, or prizes of monetary value.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, a display name ("team name"), and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, a profile photo made available by that provider.
  • Authentication data: handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). We do not see or store your password when you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Gameplay data: the game picks/predictions you submit, and your resulting win/loss record.
  • Push notification token: a device-specific token used solely to deliver notifications about your picks, game results, and league activity.
  • Feedback you submit: if you use the in-app feedback feature, the message you write is stored and visible to app administrators for support purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the core app: recording your picks, calculating results, and displaying leaderboards.
  • To send you notifications you've opted into (e.g., picks unlocking, deadlines, game results, weekly achievements).
  • To send you a weekly email confirming the picks you locked in for that week.
  • To respond to feedback or support requests.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Third-Party Services

We rely on the following service providers to operate the app. Each processes only the data necessary for its function:

  • Firebase (Google): account authentication.
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple: optional sign-in methods, at your choice.
  • Expo: push notification delivery and app update infrastructure.
  • Railway: hosts our backend server and database.
  • Resend: delivers transactional emails (e.g., your weekly picks confirmation).
  • ESPN's public sports API: we read public game scores and statistics from ESPN. Your personal data is never sent to ESPN.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active, so that leaderboards and history remain accurate. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us (below). We will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable time, except where retaining limited records is necessary to preserve the integrity of past leaderboard results for other users.

Children's Privacy

The Long Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard practices, including Firebase Authentication and encrypted connections, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
